Assessment Analytics Dashboard

Connect to your assessment backend

This dashboard is static — it has no server of its own. It fetches your data directly from your Apps Script Web App, in your browser, using the same admin key your review dashboard uses. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

The admin key is kept only in this browser tab's session storage — it is never written to this repository, never sent anywhere but your own backend, and clears when you close the tab.

Grading of written and video answers

Checking grading status…

Multiple-choice answers are graded automatically from the answer key. Written answers are first-pass scored by AI and video answers by Gemini, on the backend's 10-minute trigger (or this button). A reviewer's score always replaces the AI's.
